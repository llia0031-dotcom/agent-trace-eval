The Callow Winter Food Fair is back this weekend, and entry is free. There are forty-eight traders in Market Square this year. Every hot food stall has at least one main dish for five pounds. Bring your own container and you get fifty pence off. Stalls open at eleven in the morning and close at eight. Do not miss the free cooking demo at two in the Corn Hall. If you drive, the Mill Lane car park costs two pounds an hour. I would walk, because parking fills up fast. My plan is three dishes for under fifteen pounds. Tell me which stall I should try first.
