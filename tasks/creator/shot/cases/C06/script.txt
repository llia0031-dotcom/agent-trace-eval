Eastbrook finally has a night bus from campus to the city centre. It starts on the twenty-third of this month. It runs every twenty minutes from eleven at night until four. A single ticket costs two pounds twenty. With a valid student card you pay one pound ten. The rumour that it is free for students is not true. The last bus leaves Central Station at four in the morning. That beats a twelve pound taxi after a late shift. Do not forget to carry your student card. Would you use it? Let me know below.
