I have used the Halden desk lamp for thirty days. It costs forty-five pounds, which is not cheap. The arm folds flat, so it fits on a narrow shelf. You get five brightness levels and three colour modes. The warm mode is easy on the eyes at night. It charges by cable and lasts about eight hours. The base is heavy, so it does not tip over. My one complaint is the touch button, which is too sensitive. Do not buy it if you need a lamp for a big desk. Should I review the cheaper one next?
